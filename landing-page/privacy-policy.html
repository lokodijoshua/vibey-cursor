Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

VibeyCursor ("we", "our") is a Chrome extension plus a small web service that turns on-page designs into AI-ready prompts. This policy explains what we collect and why. It covers the MVP — if our practices change, this page will be updated.

1. What the extension accesses

  • Page content you point at. When capture mode is ON and you click an element, the extension reads that element's styles, text and layout in order to build your prompt. This happens locally in your browser.
  • Clipboard. Captured prompts are written to your clipboard so you can paste them. We never read your clipboard.
  • Local storage. Pro capture history and your license key/plan are stored in the browser's extension storage on your own device.

We run no analytics or tracking inside the extension. Your browsing activity is never sent to our servers except as described below.

2. What our servers store

  • License records (Supabase). When you buy Pro we store your email address, license key, plan, subscription status and Stripe customer/subscription IDs. On first activation we also store a random browser ID (generated by the extension, not hardware info) so a key can't be shared across browsers — reply to your license email if you need it moved to a new browser. This is the minimum needed to verify your license and email you your key.
  • Anonymous usage counting. To enforce daily capture limits (20/day free, 300/day Pro) we keep a daily counter keyed by a one-way hash of your network address (free) or your license key (Pro) — never raw addresses or keys, and never tied to your identity beyond the license record above. Counters reset every 24 hours.
  • AI enhancement requests. When you use AI Enhance, your prompt text is sent to our backend, forwarded to OpenAI for rewriting, and returned to you. Prompts are not stored. OpenAI processes them under its own API data policies (not used to train models).

3. Payments (Stripe)

Checkout is handled by Stripe on Stripe-hosted pages. We never see or store your card number. Stripe processes your payment under the Stripe Privacy Policy.

4. Emails (Resend)

We send your license key and essential purchase emails through Resend. We send no marketing email.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the processors above (Supabase, Stripe, OpenAI, Resend) as needed to run the service, or if required by law.

6. Your rights

To access, correct or delete your license record, reply to your license email or write to vibeycursorteam@gmail.com. Deleting your record deactivates Pro access.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: vibeycursorteam@gmail.com.
